Products

Six things on the bench, one idea behind them.

Each product is a different problem with the same instinct: remove complexity for the person using it. Statuses are literal.

001

Unobvious OS

Private alpha
What
A voice-first, screenless chief of staff and life planner for audio and wrist wearables. It captures fleeting thoughts, separates loose ideas from firm decisions, protects rest boundaries, and runs email, messaging and calendars in the background.
Stage
Gen-1 hardware prototyping (ESP32-S3, BLE).
Why
So you can step away from the glass.
002

Agora

Private alpha
What
An anti-algorithm, non-social reading platform. It maps your philosophical curiosities and delivers timeless essays, primary texts and contrarian ideas at a human pace.
Stage
Curation engine live.
Why
A trajectory of thought instead of an outrage feed.
003

Socratic Foundry & Youth Design Labs

Working MVP
What
Distraction-free adaptive learning tools and physical after-school and makerspace studios where learners redesign everyday systems from scratch.
Stage
Active pilots in Bengaluru.
Why
First principles over rote memorisation and pre-packaged templates.
004

Zero-Scroll Commerce

Prototype
What
An anti-clutter purchasing and replenishment engine that filters thousands of noisy listings to one high-trust, ingredient-verified food or household match, queued for one-tap approval.
Stage
Working prototype, internal testing.
Why
Choice overload is a cost.
005

Kibble & Kindred

Working MVP
What
An AI-guided daily nutrition, routine and preventive-care companion for pets, turning breed biometrics and dietary sensitivities into personalised feeding plans and automated replenishment.
Stage
Beta cohort.
Why
Consistency is a systems problem.
006

Foundry

Concept
What
A zero-middleman capability and accountability rail connecting solopreneurs and startups with verified Indian manufacturers, printers and fabricators. Plain-language ideas become standard RFQs, with milestone photo proof and automated GST invoicing.
Stage
Concept and local vendor mapping, Bengaluru.
Why
Making physical things shouldn’t depend on who you know.
