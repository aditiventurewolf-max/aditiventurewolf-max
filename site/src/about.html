About

Built for the people the giants won’t build for.

Unobvious is an independent lab in Bengaluru, run by Aditi, working on technology that gives people their attention back.

Why we are building the Unobvious

Modern technology is obsessed with maximizing revenue, efficiency, and screen time. In the rush to automate everything, the biggest labs are building for enterprises, leaving behind an entire generation of young builders and thinkers who did nothing wrong except arrive at the exact moment traditional entry-level paths disappeared.

Unobvious exists for the people the giants won't build for.

We believe the true measure of AI isn't how much profit it extracts, or how many hours it keeps you tethered to a glowing screen like a factory robot. The real point is maximizing human fulfillment: simplifying the noise of everyday life, sharpening your curiosity, and giving you the tools to build a meaningful, self-directed life on your own terms.

We build calm, ambient intelligence and everyday companions designed around one simple rule: helping humans live fuller lives, not busier ones.
