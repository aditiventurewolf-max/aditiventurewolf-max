How we work

Three rules we don’t break.

Start with an ordinary day.

We look at how people actually live before we build anything, then fix the friction we find.

Remove steps, don’t add features.

If something takes ten taps, the answer is not a better menu. It is making the ten taps unnecessary.

Ask first, then act.

Our tools never send, buy or change anything that touches other people without your say-so.

Unobvious exists for the people the giants won’t build for.
