03Entry 001 in depth

Talk to it. It sorts the mess.

Every voice note is split into loose ideas, firm decisions and how you’re doing, then routed to the right agent. Anything that touches the outside world waits for your one tap.

{{triage}}

05ActExecute, remember, go quiet
Hardware path · plan
Months 1–2Software MVPExisting earbuds and watches. Prove the triage and life-planning loop before any custom hardware.
Months 3–5Breadboard prototypeESP32-S3 or nRF5340, omnidirectional MEMS mic, tactile button, linear haptic actuator.
Months 5–7BLE burst relayVoice compressed on-chip and sent to the phone in bursts, targeting 5–7 days of battery.
Months 8–12Closed betaWaterproof enclosure, a 100-user closed beta, then hardware plus a monthly subscription.

Targets, not commitments. No ads, no engagement metrics: you pay for the utility.
