How it works · a simulated example

Say it messy. It gives you back the meaning.

“Okay so, um, that guy from yesterday, the maker thing, Rohan? Send him the proposal. The after-school one, the alternative education thing we talked about. Oh, and wait. Cat clothing. Like clothes for cats, but actually useful? Cooling, maybe? Just put it somewhere, I want to research it later. There’s also a reel in this somewhere, about how messy my head is. Make me actually film it. Aaand that fellowship, the deadline’s soon, right? Don’t let me miss it.”

  1. Messages · waits for you

    After-school proposal for Rohan

    It works out who “that guy” is, drafts it from your notes, and sends nothing until you say so.

  2. Ideas · saved

    Functional cat clothing

    A new idea, saved for research later. Nothing else happens.

  3. Planner · a nudge

    Film the reel

    A gentle prompt on Sunday, with your own line as the hook.

  4. Planner · calendar

    Fellowship deadline

    On your calendar with writing time and a reminder.

Then it goes quiet, and you look up.

Trust

It asks before it acts.

Sorting, saving and protecting your time happen quietly. Anything that reaches another person waits for one tap from you. It listens only while you press.

  • Protects your rest

Waits for your tap

  • Messages and email
  • Shared calendar invites
  • Any purchase or payment
