Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Draft pending legal review

Avaia Innovations (OPC) Private Limited (“Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), operating under the brand name Unobvious (https://www.unobvious.in), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our software and hardware products.

1. Information we collect

  • Account & contact information: name, email address, and profile details provided when you request early access, subscribe to updates, or create an account.
  • User inputs & workspace data: voice recordings, transcripts, text notes, calendar preferences, and product configurations you voluntarily submit to our ambient assistant, reader, learning, commerce, or pet wellness applications.
  • Connected third-party integrations: when you explicitly authorize integrations (such as Google Workspace, Gmail, Google Calendar, or messaging platforms), we access only the specific scopes necessary to perform your requested actions.
  • Technical & usage telemetry: basic device metadata, browser type, IP address, and error logs used strictly to maintain security and system reliability.

2. How we use your information

We process your data solely to deliver and personalize Unobvious services, process voice inputs into structured schedules and recommendations, maintain security, and respond to support inquiries. We do not sell your personal data, voice recordings, or behavioral profiles to third parties or advertisers.

3. AI processing & third-party infrastructure

To provide multimodal reasoning and cloud functionality, we use enterprise-grade cloud and AI infrastructure providers, including Google Cloud Platform (Vertex AI) and Anthropic. Data transmitted to these processors is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, and is governed by enterprise data-processing agreements that prohibit using your private inputs to train public foundation models.

4. Data retention & your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

We retain personal data only as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Under applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent for connected integrations at any time.
