## 1. The idea

> **Everything else can blur.**

Unobvious builds quiet technology. The identity rests on one optical idea:

- **A sharp frame inside soft focus.** The frame is the product's job: sorting the noise of a day down to what matters.
- **One warm light in a dark room.** The light is the press: one button, one moment of attention, then you look up.

Every decision below comes from those two sentences. If something doesn't serve either, cut it.

### Where it came from

These are the founder's references, and what each one gave us:

| Reference | What we took |
|---|---|
| Pinterest: grainy blur, silhouettes in haze, "Midnight Amber" gradient pack | Grain, soft focus, light as the subject |
| Palette pins: Midnight Amber (#13181B, #003A6C, #FFBF65, #FD8973, #F0EEEB) and Cinema (#212121, #AA1F23, #EE2324, #EDE1D3) | The two palettes |
| "Focus" poster, OPAC identity, orange-frame poster | Bracket frame; only what's inside it is sharp |
| "Thoughts are not real, but they are loud" | Noise turned into an image |
| United Carriers, Worldline | Scroll moves a camera through one object |
| Perplexity Computer | Calm editorial restraint, soft dissolves |
| Serene | Large quiet type over a full-bleed image |

### What we tried and moved past

Kept in the prototype switcher for comparison:

| Home | Name | Why we moved on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First Light | Paper-and-orange editorial |
| 2 | Index | A list |
| 3 | The Button | A pressable object |
| 4 | The Journey | A scroll story; good mechanics, too plain |
| 5 | Serene | Taste test; third-party video |
| 6 | Poster | Poster type woven through the product; strong, but cold for the brand |

Their shared lesson: **a real object and real light beat abstract shapes.**

## 2. Logo

### Routes explored

Shown side by side in `brand.html`:

| Route | Concept |
|---|---|
| **Focus** (recommended) | Four corner brackets around one warm dot. The frame keeps what matters; the dot is the press. Explains the company in a shape, holds up at 16 px, and is also the interface's focus frame. |
| Halo | An off-centre light inside a ring, like an eclipse. Softer and more atmospheric, less literal. |
| Soft o | A wordmark only, with one "o" out of focus. Witty, but needs a symbol for small sizes. |
| Look up | A light rising over a horizon line. The product's promise, but generic as a shape. |
| The press | The device as an icon: body, button, status light. Most specific, least flexible as the company grows beyond one product. |

### The Focus mark

**Geometry** (64-unit grid):
- **Brackets:** square-capped, 4-unit stroke, 14-unit arms, inset 8 units.
- **Dot:** radius 9, centred.

**Lockup:** mark plus "Unobvious" in Inter Tight 600 at −0.04em. Gap between mark and word is about one dot width. A stacked version is allowed.

**Colour:**

| Setting | Frame | Dot |
|---|---|---|
| On night | Moonlight | Amber |
| On moonlight | Night | Ember #E8590C (amber is too light on paper) |
| One-colour | Same as frame | Same as frame |

**Clear space and size:**
- Clear space is x = dot diameter on all sides.
- Minimum size is 16 px for the mark and 72 px wide for the lockup.

**Don'ts:**
- Don't blur the mark.
- Don't recolour it outside the table above.
- Don't rotate it.
- Don't add dots.

**Files** in `assets/brand/`:
- `mark.svg`
- `mark-ink.svg`
- `lockup-dark.svg`
- `lockup-light.svg`
- `favicon.svg`

> **To do before print:** the lockup SVGs set the word as live text in Inter Tight. Outline the word to paths in a vector tool before sending to print or using where the font isn't loaded.

## 3. Colour

### Midnight Amber (primary)

| Token | Hex | Role | Text on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | `#13181B` | Primary ground | Moonlight · 15.44:1 |
| Moonlight | `#F0EEEB` | Text on night, light pages | Night · 15.44:1 |
| Amber | `#FFBF65` | The light. Primary action | Night · 10.98:1 |
| Coral | `#FD8973` | Warmth in plates | Night · 7.67:1 (atmosphere, not body text) |
| Atlantic | `#003A6C` | Depth in plates | Moonlight · 9.95:1 |
| Fog | `#CCD5DA` | Quiet surfaces | Night · 12.01:1 |
| Ember | `#E8590C` | The dot and small accents on light grounds | — |

### Cinema (alternate)

`#151515` · `#EDE1D3` · `#EE2324` · `#AA1F23`. Use it for campaigns and launches, not the everyday site.

### Rules

- **Amber is the light.** One amber action per screen.
- **Never amber text on moonlight.** It fails contrast. Use ember for small accents on light grounds.
- **Measured, not guessed.** Contrast in `brand.html` and on the Home 7 day tiles is computed live from the actual colours. Keep it that way.

## 5. Light plates (backgrounds)

**What a plate is.** A grainy, soft-focus light field: a few large light sources on Night, blended in linear light, with film grain. They're ours, deterministic, and regenerated with `python3 src/make_backgrounds.py`.

| Plate | Use |
|---|---|
| `halo` / `halo-cinema` | Behind the product (square, the ring frames the device) |
| `amber-horizon` | Openings and closes |
| `amber-wave` | Motion moments |
| `amber-window` | Interiors, the day, noise |
| `amber-eclipse` | Section backdrops (dimmed) |
| `paper-glow` | Light pages |
| `cinema-veil` | Cinema palette, all dark sections |

**Rules:**
- **One plate per section.** Plates breathe slowly (26s scale drift); they never move with the cursor.
- **Text over a plate.** Darken behind text with a gradient scrim, or keep text over the plate's dark side.
- **Don't fix contrast with a grey veil.** That kills the glow.

## 6. Imagery

1. **Soft focus, real grain.** People and places slightly out of focus. The moment, not the face.
2. **One warm light per image:** window, lamp or status light.
3. **Frame what matters.** Brackets, not boxes. Only the framed thing is sharp.

**The product render.** `assets/media/device-hero.webp` and the 48-frame turntable `assets/media/spin/` are an original 3D render (three.js, rendered offline). They are a **design stand-in, not the real prototype**. Replace them with real photography or a render of the real device when it exists.

**Wanted:** real grainy photos (hands, a walk, a window, the workspace) shot to the rules above.

## 7. Motion

**Principles:**
- **Motion is focus.** Things sharpen as they arrive and blur as they leave. Use slow eases (`cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, 0.8–1.6s). Nothing bounces.
- **Scroll is a camera.** On the web, scrolling moves toward the button, through the noise, into the light.

**Signature moments on Home 7:**
- **Focus frame:** follows the pointer; sharp inside, blurred outside.
- **Push-in:** the camera enters the press button, zooming about its measured centre (14.6%, 56.1% of the render).
- **Noise field:** blurs away as three kept items come into focus.

**Fallbacks:**
- **Reduced motion:** every page is complete and still. Motion is opt-in via a class set only when reduced motion is off.
- **No JavaScript:** content stays visible.

## 8. Voice

Calm, plain, on your side. Short sentences, ordinary words. Say what happens, and what we won't do.

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| It asks before it acts. | Unleash your productivity |
| Press once, then look up. | Revolutionary AI-powered assistant |
| Your rest is protected. | Never miss a thing |
| We never sell your data. | 10x your day |
| Small on purpose. | Join thousands of users |

**Never invent** metrics, testimonials, logos, users or awards. Hardware specifics are private.

## 9. Layout and components

- **Grid:** max width 1280px, gutter `clamp(20px, 4.5vw, 64px)`, 8px spacing rhythm.
- **Buttons:** pill-shaped with mono labels. Amber fill for the one primary action; outline for secondary.
- **Hairlines over boxes:** structure comes from 1px rules, not cards with shadows.
- **Corner metadata:** small mono labels at section edges ("focus", "look up", coordinates).
- **Accessibility:**
  - Contrast is AA or better, measured.
  - Focus rings are visible in amber.
  - No horizontal scroll at 360px.
