About

An independent studio for the people the giants won’t build for.

Unobvious is a studio working at the edge of applied AI, hardware and systems design. We draw on old-school design: Bell Labs monographs, Braun industrial design, the idea that a good product explains itself and then gets out of the way.

We start with Unobvious OS, a calm, voice-first companion. Around it, a lab of other systems and products, all asking the same question: how does this make a person’s life simpler instead of more complicated?

The measure we care about is human fulfilment, not engagement or extraction. We make money from direct utility, through tactile hardware and subscriptions, never from your attention.
